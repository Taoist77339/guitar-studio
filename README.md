# Practice Studio

A complete beginner acoustic guitar course that runs in the browser and installs like a regular app.

**Open it:** https://taoist77339.github.io/guitar-studio/ — then choose **Install** (desktop Chrome/Edge) or **Add to Home Screen** (phone). Works offline once installed.

## The course: 8 units, 40 lessons

1. **Getting started** — parts of the guitar, posture, tuning, reading chord charts and tab, first notes
2. **Your first chords** — Em, Asus2, Am, E, D, A, C, G, one-minute changes, first three-chord song
3. **Rhythm and strumming** — counting, down-up eighths, the folk strum, chunks, 3/4 waltz, changing in rhythm
4. **Melody and picking** — first melodies, alternate picking (spider), C major scale, hammer-ons and pull-offs
5. **More chords** — Dm, Fmaj7, seventh chords, the anchored G family, sus embellishments, walking bass
6. **Fingerstyle** — thumb and fingers, Travis picking, 6/8 arpeggios, pinches
7. **Barre chords and beyond** — power chords, the F barre, E- and A-shape barres, the capo
8. **Musicianship** — fretboard notes, keys and numbers, minor pentatonic, 12-bar blues, playing by ear

Every lesson has something to play: chord diagrams you can hear, play-along tracks with a count-in and adjustable tempo, tabs that play back, fretboard maps, a one-minute chord-change timer, and ear-training quizzes. Progress is saved on your device.

**Play with mic:** every tab and play-along has a *Play with mic* mode. Notes and strums scroll along six string lanes toward a hit line while the app listens through your microphone. Each one is scored for timing (Perfect / Good / early / late), pitch (cents sharp or flat, or which wrong note you played) and, for strums, whether the chord's notes are there. A live panel shows the note you're playing, a pitch needle against the target and the notes heard. Score, streak multiplier, accuracy and average timing update as you play, and a timing correction compensates for your device's audio delay.

**Tools:** live microphone tuner, 39-chord library, and a jam mode for your own progressions.

## Files

No build step: `index.html` (app + audio engine), `course.js` (all lesson content), a manifest and a service worker.
